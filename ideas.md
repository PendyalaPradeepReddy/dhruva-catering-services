## Selected Direction: Telangana Feast Editorial

### Design Movement
Contemporary **Indian editorial hospitality**: a poised collision of premium restaurant photography, wedding stationery proportions, and the spirited colour language of a festive Telangana meal.

### Core Principles
1. **Food as the hero:** Large, text-safe photographs of buffets and live-counter craft carry the emotional weight; copy is purposeful and never competes with food.
2. **Ceremony in the details:** Fine gold rules, scalloped food-window shapes, and petite service labels make each section feel prepared rather than templated.
3. **Confident asymmetry:** Content follows an editorial column and offset-image layout, avoiding a repetitive stack of centered boxes.
4. **Useful celebration:** The page privileges real booking decisions—event scale, cuisine categories, 24/7 availability, and direct enquiry paths.

### Color Philosophy
**Deep maroon** grounds the brand in occasion and trust. **Saffron** creates appetite and action, while a controlled **antique gold** functions as a ceremonial trace rather than a shiny default. A gently warm **rice-paper off-white** provides visual rest and preserves the rich photography’s contrast.

### Layout Paradigm
An **editorial feast table**: page sections alternate between full-bleed photography and paper-like content fields. A slim left-side “occasion index” appears on wider screens, while staggered image blocks interrupt the reading rhythm. Cards are used sparingly only for functional information (scale and menu tabs), never as a uniform page grid.

### Signature Elements
1. A subtle **banana-leaf line motif** that marks section starts and dividers.
2. **Saffron reservation markers**—small bordered labels with uppercase category text.
3. **Scalloped image corners** inspired by a plated thali rim and traditional serving platters.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interaction should feel like being guided by a gracious host. Navigation is direct, phone and WhatsApp actions are persistent, and menu tabs switch without page reload. Links use a subtle saffron underline travel; buttons have a firm press response rather than a bouncy effect.

### Animation
At entry, only the hero type, category flag, and lead image emerge in a 450ms stagger using opacity and a 10px vertical transform. On scroll, section labels and food images reveal once at 320–450ms with a warm cubic-bezier ease. Hover movement is constrained to 2–4px and 180ms. Respect reduced-motion preferences by disabling all non-essential movement.

### Typography System
**Playfair Display** is used for display headlines in semi-bold and italic accent phrases; **DM Sans** provides clear, friendly body copy and utility text. Headings use generous tracking only in small uppercase labels; long text stays compact and highly legible. Nothing uses Inter.

### Brand Essence
**Dhruva Catering Services brings composed, generous Indian hospitality from fifty guests to five thousand across Hanamkonda and beyond.**

**Personality:** hospitable, dependable, celebratory.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are assured and sensory; CTAs are plain-speaking and action-oriented. Microcopy is attentive about practical details such as guest count, timing, and region.

> “A memorable table begins with thoughtful service.”

> “Tell us your occasion. We’ll shape the menu around it.”

### Wordmark & Logo
A compact saffron **D** formed from a folded banana leaf, framed by a maroon serving-dome arc. It is a standalone visual symbol; the wordmark pairs the mark with Playfair Display in a tight, tailored lockup.

### Signature Brand Color
**Dhruva Saffron — #F28C28**

## Style Decisions

- Primary food imagery uses visibly arched, platter-inspired **scalloped food windows**, not generic rounded cards.
- Every inner route includes a substantial food, buffet, or service-process image so the hosting craft remains tangible throughout the site.
- Dhruva Saffron is reserved for decisive actions, petite reservation labels, and key highlights. Large saffron fields are balanced by maroon, rice paper, and antique-gold rules.
